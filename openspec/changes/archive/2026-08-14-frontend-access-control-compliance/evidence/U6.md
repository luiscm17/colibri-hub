## Scope and Rollback

The Access-private gate normalizes role IDs, blocks zero-delta previews, binds a ready preview to subject/version/authority generation, and consumes the preview version for one apply request. Draft edits, authority changes, and failures invalidate the gate; a consumed apply request cannot be replayed.
